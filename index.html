    Reshape Scope & Boundary
    Because this mode strictly preserves 100% of your playlist’s original tracks without injecting external songs, resequencing operates within a fixed song pool. Arc calculates the closest mathematical sequence to your target energy curve. However, because the available songs are fixed, steep energy peaks or sudden valleys might not mirror your shape 100% if the playlist lacks tracks at those exact energy levels.
